Voronoi diagrams, two ways

Fortune's sweep-line algorithm and a divide-and-conquer construction compute the same partition of the plane with very different strategies. Step through each one below, or click the canvas to add your own points.

Click the canvas to add a point.

Fortune's sweep-line

The sweep line moves down. Each arc of the beach line is a parabola with a site as its focus. Breakpoints between arcs trace Voronoi edges; when an arc shrinks to nothing, a vertex appears.

Divide & conquer

Sites are sorted by x and split at the median. Each half is solved recursively, then the left and right diagrams are merged by tracing the dividing chain between them.

Single-run timings on this point set, for a quick sanity check.

Correctness tests

Each case checks that both algorithms find the same neighbouring sites, that they match an independent O(n³) brute-force test for small inputs, that every Voronoi vertex is equidistant from its three sites, and that every edge lies on the right bisector. Degenerate inputs (collinear, co-circular, shared y) are included.
